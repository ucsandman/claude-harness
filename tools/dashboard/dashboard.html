🚀 What is DashClaw & Why Do You Need It? Governed Autonomy

An AI agent with credentials can deploy, delete, pay, and publish. The failure mode is not malice; it is an agent doing something expensive, irreversible, or catastrophic because nothing stood between its intent and the world. DashClaw is that runtime: it intercepts risky agent actions before execution, routes sensitive decisions to your phone or browser for one-click approval, and records a cryptographically signed evidence trail.

1. 🛡️ Guard (Policy Pre-Check)
Evaluates tool intent against policies (risk score, regex, limits). Returns allow, warn, require_approval, or block.
2. 📱 Remote Approvals
Paused tool calls wait safely while you approve from Web UI, CLI, Phone PWA, Telegram, or Discord.
3. 📜 Cryptographic Audit
Every action, assumption, and outcome is sealed in an immutable ledger with full decision replay.
4. 🔒 Fail-Closed & Standalone
If remote DashClaw is offline, local safety guards take over automatically to protect secret files & DBs.
